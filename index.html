    Nightly weights 8 PM · grams
    Tap a cell to enter or correct a weight in grams. Italic with “est.” = estimated from the printed graph; dashed = no weight yet. Typing over a value replaces it (Undo appears); clearing a cell deletes it.

    Milk at the NICU Estimate

    The estimate is not a count. It starts from the NICU's last count (or your first drop-off), adds drop-offs since, and subtracts each baby's full feed at every scheduled feed time since. Update it whenever the nurses give you a real number.
