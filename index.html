          Support for parents

          • National Maternal Mental Health Hotline — call or text 1-833-TLC-MAMA (1-833-852-6262), 24/7, free and confidential. Support for pregnant and new parents and their families.
            CallText
          • Postpartum Support International HelpLine — call or text 1-800-944-4773 (Spanish text: 971-203-7773). Leave a message; a trained volunteer gets back to you, usually during business hours. Not for emergencies. Free online groups, including groups for NICU parents.
            CallTextpostpartum.net
          • Hand to Hold — free peer mentors (parents who've been through the NICU) and support groups, in English and Spanish.
            handtohold.org
          • Your NICU's social worker — every NICU has one. Ask your baby's nurse to connect you; they can help with stress, money, lodging and getting home.

          These are independent services, not part of this app. This app doesn't read or send anything about your check-ins.
