Privacy Policy

NICUlog · Effective November 20, 2026

NICUlog helps parents keep track of their babies' feeds, weights and milk supply during a NICU stay. This page explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What the app stores

  • Your account: your email address. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; the app never sees or stores it.
  • What your family enters: your babies' names, birth date and time, birth weight and gestational age; feeds, weights and notes; pumping and milk drop-offs; checklists, plans and questions for rounds; and the display names you choose.
  • Photos you add to a moment: each photo is resized on your phone before it is saved (at most 1600 pixels on the long side, plus a small preview). Resizing also removes its location and camera details. Everyone in your family can see your photos. The original stays on your phone; the app doesn't keep it.
  • Your private check-ins (More → Support): how you're doing and an optional note. Only you can read them; no one else in your family can, and the app's developer does not look at them. They are stored with the rest of your family's data and encrypted in transit and at rest, but they are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Check-ins you choose to share: sharing puts a copy in your family's journey, where everyone in your family can see it. Unsharing removes the copy.
  • Who entered each item: the email of the family member who saved it, so you both can see who logged what.
  • Preferences on this phone (text size, appearance, units) stay on the device and are never uploaded.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no selling or renting of your data.
  • Nothing is sent to your hospital or anyone else. Only you decide who joins your family.
  • Check-ins are never scored, never trigger alerts, and are never sent anywhere. Nothing on any other screen changes because of them.
  • The support resources (More → Support) are phone numbers and links to independent services, such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. They are part of the app itself, so they work with no signal; the app shares nothing with them. If you call, text or visit one, that service's own privacy practices apply.

Who can see your data

Only the people in your family, meaning those who joined with an invite code you shared. Security rules on the server stop anyone else from reading or changing it. The developer does not look at your family's data unless you ask for help and give permission.

Where it is kept

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), which encrypts it in transit and at rest. Google acts as our service provider and processes the data only to run the app. The app also keeps an offline copy on your phone so it works without signal.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete my account (More → Settings → Family) removes your sign-in. If you are the last person in your family, all of the family's data is deleted too.
  • Leave this family removes your access; the other members keep the data.
  • When you leave a family, are removed from it, or delete your account, your private check-ins are deleted. Copies you shared stay in the family's journey.
  • Deleting a photo, or the moment it belongs to, removes it from your family's data.
  • Backup files you save from the app stay wherever you put them, under your control. A backup file lists your photos but doesn't contain the images.

Children

The app is for parents and caregivers. It stores information about infants only as entered by their parents, and it is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

NICUlog is a family tracking aid. Your NICU team's charting and orders are the source of truth.
