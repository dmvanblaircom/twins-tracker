Privacy Policy

Twins Tracker · Effective November 15, 2026

Twins Tracker helps parents keep track of their babies' feeds, weights and milk supply during a NICU stay. This page explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What the app stores

  • Your account: your email address. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; the app never sees or stores it.
  • What your family enters: your babies' names, birth date and time, birth weight and gestational age; feeds, weights and notes; pumping and milk drop-offs; checklists, plans and questions for rounds; and the display names you choose.
  • Who entered each item: the email of the family member who saved it, so you both can see who logged what.
  • Preferences on this phone (text size, appearance, units) stay on the device and are never uploaded.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no selling or renting of your data.
  • Nothing is sent to your hospital or anyone else. Only you decide who joins your family.

Who can see your data

Only the people in your family, meaning those who joined with an invite code you shared. Security rules on the server stop anyone else from reading or changing it. The developer does not look at your family's data unless you ask for help and give permission.

Where it is kept

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), which encrypts it in transit and at rest. Google acts as our service provider and processes the data only to run the app. The app also keeps an offline copy on your phone so it works without signal.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete my account (More → Settings → Family) removes your sign-in. If you are the last person in your family, all of the family's data is deleted too.
  • Leave this family removes your access; the other members keep the data.
  • Backup files you save from the app stay wherever you put them, under your control.

Children

The app is for parents and caregivers. It stores information about infants only as entered by their parents, and it is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

Twins Tracker is a family tracking aid. Your NICU team's charting and orders are the source of truth.
